Why desktop shows twice the impressions of mobile, with the same clicks

Desktop: 113k impressions at an average position of 34. Mobile: 54k impressions at position 19. Both got about 675 clicks. On desktop, Google counts it as an impression even when you appear far down the results (pages 3–7), where almost nobody looks. Mobile better reflects how you really rank, and there it does fine. Nothing is broken.

Locations

Santee ranks well; Downtown and Oceanside should too

Santee pages land on page one or two. Downtown and Oceanside pages sit around position 40–57, even though both are real shops. /city/oceanside/ was shown 33,500 times at position 45.7 and got almost no clicks. Santee likely has the stronger Google Business Profile. Downtown and Oceanside need the same setup.

  • Give each shop its own Google Business Profile, linked to its own page on the site.
  • On each shop page, show the address, hours, phone number, a map and photos of that shop, matching the profile exactly.
  • Make each shop's city page its main page, and link the service pages for that city (embroidery, engraving…) to it.
  • For areas without a shop (La Jolla, Chula Vista, Coronado…), say “served from our Downtown shop” and link there. Improve or hide the thinnest ones. They rank 59–72 and get no clicks.
Housekeeping

Technical cleanup

  • Test and extra versions of the site are in Google. staging., mail. and design.imageonsd.com are competing with the real site. Hide them from Google or password-protect them.
  • Duplicate addresses: some pages load both with and without a trailing slash (/services/engraving vs /services/engraving/). Redirect one to the other.
  • Stray keyword tags: Google is matching the site to “embroidery san diego #local #geo #san-diego #high-priority”. Some tool or page is publishing internal keyword notes, and they should be removed.
  • New page in progress: “DTG & DTF vs. Screen Printing” for people searching screen printing (about 2,200 impressions over six months, some already near page one).
